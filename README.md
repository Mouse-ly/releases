# Mousely for Windows

Turn your iPhone into a wireless trackpad for your Windows PC. Swipe to move the cursor, tap to click (1 finger = left, 2 fingers = right). Connects over Wi-Fi with Bluetooth fallback.

## Download

**[Download Mousely for Windows](https://github.com/Mouse-ly/releases/releases/latest/download/Mouse-ly-Setup.exe)** (Windows 10/11)

You also need the iPhone app, free on TestFlight: **[testflight.apple.com/join/R3ykgp1m](https://testflight.apple.com/join/R3ykgp1m)**

## "Windows protected your PC"?

The beta installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen shows a blue warning the first time. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. The installer is per-user (no admin rights needed).

## Setup

1. Install Mousely on your PC (link above) and launch it
2. Install the iPhone app from TestFlight (or scan the QR in the desktop onboarding)
3. Open the app on both devices, the onboarding walks you through pairing
